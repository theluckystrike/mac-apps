Starglass Privacy Policy

Starglass shows a stargazing forecast in the menu bar.

When you search for a city, the city name you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service. To get the forecast, the selected coordinates are sent to the Open-Meteo forecast service. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Your saved location, settings and the last forecast stay on your Mac. The app does not use your device location.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
