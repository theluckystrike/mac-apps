PageParcel support

PageParcel plans the order and physical sheet sides of an ordered PDF packet.

Get started

Open Sample mode to try the workflow with synthetic local data. Sample results are labeled and remain separate from your live work.

Choose PDFs, arrange them in packet order, and mark documents that must start on a front side. Review the assembly table under the displayed duplex assumption.

Understand the result

Planned blanks are virtual. The app does not merge or change PDFs and does not send jobs to a printer. Locked, unreadable, or empty files block a complete plan. A changed page count requires review before replacing an expectation.

If access to a selected file or folder expires, choose it again and rescan. Review the latest check time and coverage before relying on a result. Clear local data removes app state and saved access without deleting your source files.
