PageParcel Privacy Policy

Michael Lip provides PageParcel. The app plans the order and physical sheet sides of an ordered PDF packet. All app processing takes place on your Mac.

Local data

Selected PDF paths, access bookmarks, document labels, expected page counts, packet order, front side rules, and saved summaries stay in the app sandbox container. PDF counts and source page previews are processed locally. The app does not save copies of the source PDFs.

Selected input files remain in their original locations and are not modified. Saved access bookmarks let the app reopen only the files or folders you authorize. Removing a project, plan, or selected root removes its saved references. Clear local data removes saved app state and bookmarks without deleting source files.

Collection and sharing

The app does not collect data from you. It has no analytics, ads, accounts, cloud sync, telemetry, or automatic network requests. Sample mode uses synthetic local fixtures and separate saved state.

Assembly sheets contain document order, labels, source page counts, planned blanks, page ranges, assumptions, and unresolved files. Source page content is not part of the text report. Copying a report places the displayed content on the system clipboard only when you choose Copy. Other apps may access the clipboard. Your later sharing is outside this app.

Opening Support or Privacy launches your browser at the relevant page on barframe.com. Your browser may make a normal web request. If you contact support, the email or GitHub service receives the information you send. GitHub issues are public.

Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email lipmichal@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue.
