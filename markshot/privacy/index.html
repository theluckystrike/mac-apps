MarkShot Privacy Policy

MarkShot captures screenshots and lets you annotate them (arrows, text, highlights, blur).

It captures the screen only when you ask, with the Screen Recording permission you grant. Images stay on your Mac unless you copy, save or share them.

You can remove Screen Recording access in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
