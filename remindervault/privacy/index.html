ReminderVault Privacy Policy

ReminderVault syncs tasks between an Obsidian vault folder that you choose and Apple Reminders on your Mac.

It reads and writes only the vault folder you select (with your permission) and the Reminders lists you allow. Sync happens only on your Mac.

You can remove Reminders access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Reminders.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
