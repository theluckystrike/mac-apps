StepQuay Privacy Policy

Michael Lip provides StepQuay. Tasks, resume notes, timing records, history, and settings stay in the app sandbox container on your Mac. The app does not make network requests, run analytics, require an account, or sync data to a cloud service.

StepQuay does not request access to your calendar, files, microphone, or Accessibility controls. Its global shortcut opens its own action panel. Sample mode uses fictional tasks and keeps them separate from your live queue.

You can clear session history independently of the current queue in Settings.

Opening a support or privacy link launches your default browser. The destination website receives the normal browser request and uses its own website privacy policy.

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email lipmichal@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue.
