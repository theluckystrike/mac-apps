StepQuay support

Use Try Sample to explore fictional data without configuring live inputs.

Add an action with a title and a positive effort estimate, then start it from the queue. Interrupting an action saves a resume note and pauses its clock. After sleep, resume the action explicitly.

The available time budget previews which consecutive actions fit. A timer reaching its estimate does not complete the action. Use Clear history in Settings to remove session history independently of the current queue.

If the global shortcut cannot be registered, choose another shortcut in Settings or open the panel from the menu bar.
