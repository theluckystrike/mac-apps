MeetNext Privacy Policy

MeetNext reads your calendars on this Mac (with your permission) to show your next meeting and today's timeline.

Event data is processed only on your Mac and is never uploaded. When you click Join, the meeting link opens in your browser or meeting app.

You can remove calendar access at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
