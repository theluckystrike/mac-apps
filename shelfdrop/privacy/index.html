ShelfDrop Privacy Policy

ShelfDrop keeps files, text and images that you drag onto it in a temporary shelf.

Shelf items and their access bookmarks stay on your Mac. Files are opened only when you drop or select them.

When you choose Share, macOS sends the item to the service you pick (for example AirDrop or Mail).

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
