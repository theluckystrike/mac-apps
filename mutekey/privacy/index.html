MuteKey Privacy Policy

MuteKey mutes and unmutes your Mac's microphone with a keyboard shortcut and shows the state in the menu bar.

MuteKey changes the mute or volume setting of your input device. It does not record, store or transmit audio.

Your shortcut, settings and per-device preferences stay on your Mac.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
