RevPulse Privacy Policy

RevPulse shows your Stripe revenue in the menu bar.

You enter a Stripe restricted, read-only API key. It is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. The app sends requests with this key directly from your Mac to api.stripe.com. Stripe's privacy policy applies to that data.

Revenue figures are cached only on your Mac.

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any data or keys. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
